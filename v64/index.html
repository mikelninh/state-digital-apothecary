01

The alignment map

Four interests.
One honest system.

Most harmful products blur the people who use, pay for, benefit from and build them. STATE names each party before making a product or contract decision.

USER ≠ BUYER
BUYER ≠ BENEFICIARY
01

The person

Uses the formula, shares vulnerable context and bears the direct experience.

Needs agency, privacy, safety and an easy exit.
02

The buyer

Pays for access, delivery, implementation or evaluation.

Needs credible value without control over private inner life.
03

The beneficiary

May include a family, class, team, patient group or community.

Needs real-world improvement and equitable access.
04

The builder

Designs, reviews, facilitates, funds or maintains the system.

Needs fair pay, clear rights and bounded responsibility.
A deal is aligned only when no party’s gain depends on another party becoming less free.STATE DEAL FILTER 01
02

Six non-negotiable promises

What STATE
will not trade away.

These promises apply to consumer products, workplace pilots, school programmes, health partnerships and public initiatives.

PRODUCT · SALES
DATA · GOVERNANCE
PROMISE 01

Freedom over retention

No streak shame, infinite feed, manipulative urgency or contract metric based on screen time.

Success can include using STATE less.
PROMISE 02

Privacy over managerial curiosity

Employers, schools and institutions never receive identifiable emotional or practice data by default.

Aggregate reporting must resist re-identification.
PROMISE 03

Conditions over coping theatre

STATE will not help institutions merely train people to tolerate preventable harm.

Every programme can recommend environmental change.
PROMISE 04

Evidence over spectacle

Claims remain sourced, bounded and correctable. Personal observations are not presented as universal proof.

No claim ships without limits.
PROMISE 05

Payment over participation theatre

Contributors receive clear scope, acceptance criteria, attribution and compensation before work begins.

Tokens and receipts never replace enforceable rights.
PROMISE 06

Exit over lock-in

People can pause, stop, delete, export or leave. Institutional access does not become behavioural coercion.

The open door is a product requirement.

04

Metric constitution

What we reward
shapes what we become.

Metrics are not neutral. STATE separates healthy commercial outcomes from useful but dangerous proxies and measures it refuses entirely.

PAY FOR · WATCH
REFUSE

Pay for

Aligned outcomes

  • Safe programme delivery
  • Completed finite practices
  • Real-world actions
  • Equitable access
  • Facilitator capability
  • Validated outcome improvement
  • Increasing self-sufficiency

Track carefully

Useful proxies

  • Voluntary return
  • Session completion
  • Self-reported state shift
  • Next-hour usefulness
  • Team climate
  • Absence and retention
  • Wearable signals

Refuse

Misaligned incentives

  • Minutes watched
  • Longest streak
  • Mandatory participation
  • Individual emotion dashboards
  • Manager access to notes
  • Bonuses for stronger claims
  • Revenue tied to dependency

THE ECONOMIC CONSTITUTION

STATE will not become rich by making people feel inadequate, exposing their vulnerability, or teaching them to endure conditions that should change.

We build finite tools, fair collaborations and institutions capable of creating healthier bodies, calmer minds and more loving relationships. When incentives drift, this charter should make the conflict visible before growth makes it harder to correct.
