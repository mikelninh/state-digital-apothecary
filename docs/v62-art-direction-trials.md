# STATE V6.2 — Art Direction Trials

## Purpose

V6.2 does not add product scope. It tests which visual world can carry the existing STATE philosophy with enough distinctiveness, warmth and daily usefulness.

All three directions use the same signature moment:

1. arrive overwhelmed;
2. receive recognition before advice;
3. receive CALM 10;
4. complete a 60-second guided-exhale preview;
5. leave STATE without an upsell or next recommendation.

Keeping the content constant makes the visual comparison more honest.

## Direction A — The Living Apothecary

**Visual language**

- warm ceramic and tactile vessels;
- paper labels and editorial typography;
- sunlight, wood and botanical linework;
- evidence notes presented like careful dispensary labels.

**Strengths**

- desirable and approachable;
- makes formulas feel tangible;
- strongest fit with the digital-apothecary origin;
- easy to extend into physical products and packaging.

**Risks**

- may resemble premium lifestyle and skincare brands;
- needs stronger proprietary symbols to avoid an Aesop-like visual category;
- can over-romanticise the drug metaphor.

## Direction B — The Inner Observatory

**Visual language**

- dark contemplative space;
- state constellations and orbital systems;
- scientific instrument labels;
- measured, inferred and unmeasured signals shown separately.

**Strengths**

- most distinctive and futuristic;
- strong home for science, personal data and mystery;
- makes invisible conditions visually explorable;
- strong digital-native identity.

**Risks**

- may feel cold during vulnerable moments;
- can drift toward generic neuroscience or sci-fi;
- needs warmth and human texture for relationships and family use.

## Direction C — The House That Breathes

**Visual language**

- real rooms, windows, light and furniture;
- architecture changes with the user’s condition;
- formula objects live inside the environment;
- the exit remains a central design element.

**Strengths**

- best embodiment of “build the conditions in which life can flourish”;
- can make Body, Mind and Relationships spatially intuitive;
- emotionally warm and suitable for everyday return;
- naturally supports the anti-addiction open-door principle.

**Risks**

- highest art and engineering cost;
- risks becoming illustrative or childlike if execution lacks restraint;
- requires a coherent architectural system across many states.

## Team hypothesis

The likely final system is not a flat compromise:

- **C** provides the primary world and navigation;
- **A** provides the formula objects, packaging and tactile desirability;
- **B** provides science, evidence, personal data and contemplative depth.

V6.2 still requires the founder to experience each direction before this combination becomes a decision.

## Decision scorecard

Rate each direction from 1–10 on:

1. Would I return here during an ordinary difficult day?
2. Do I understand what to do without explanation?
3. Does this feel unmistakably like STATE?
4. Does the design make the intervention more useful?
5. Does it respect vulnerability rather than perform wellness?
6. Can I imagine Body, Mind and Relationships living here?
7. Does the ending make me want to leave the screen?
8. Would I be proud to show this to a researcher, school, employer and friend?

## Decision gate

Do not lock the art direction until:

- all three prototypes have been used end-to-end;
- at least five external testers have ranked them;
- one direction wins on both return desire and willingness to leave;
- the winner can be implemented accessibly on mobile;
- the visual metaphor remains compatible with the source standard and anti-addiction constitution.

> The product has found its soul. V6.2 searches for the body that can carry it.
