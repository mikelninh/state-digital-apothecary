# STATE Incentive Alignment Charter

> **The business should become stronger when the human becomes freer.**

Status: Draft 01 — public, challengeable and revisable.

## Purpose

STATE exists to help people discover and create the conditions for healthy bodies, calm minds and loving relationships. Its products, contracts, metrics and governance must not reward dependency, surveillance, coercion or preventable harm.

## Alignment map

Before a product or contract decision, name:

1. **The person** using the product and bearing the direct experience.
2. **The buyer** paying for access, delivery, implementation or evaluation.
3. **The beneficiary** whose body, relationships, team, school, family or community may be affected.
4. **The builder** designing, reviewing, facilitating, funding or maintaining the system.

A deal is aligned only when no party's gain depends on another party becoming less free.

## Six non-negotiable promises

### 1. Freedom over retention

- No infinite feed, streak shame, manipulative urgency or screen-time target.
- Missing a day is not failure.
- Using STATE less can be a successful outcome.
- Every formula has a visible ending and exit.

### 2. Privacy over managerial curiosity

- Employers, schools and institutions do not receive identifiable emotional states, notes or individual practice histories by default.
- Aggregate reports must use thresholds and controls that reduce re-identification risk.
- Private data is not sold as an institutional insight product.

### 3. Conditions over coping theatre

- STATE does not merely help people tolerate preventable harm.
- Workplace, school, health and public programmes may recommend changes to workload, recovery, management, environment, access, routines or relationships.
- Institutions share responsibility for the conditions they create.

### 4. Evidence over spectacle

- Claims are sourced, bounded, dated and correctable.
- Evidence strength, limitations and possible harms remain visible.
- Personal patterns are observations, not universal proof.
- STATE does not imply that a phone measured hormones, neurotransmitters or brain activation.

### 5. Payment over participation theatre

- Contributors receive scope, acceptance criteria, decision ownership, payment, attribution and dispute terms before work begins.
- Rejected work is not used without permission and payment.
- Tokens, receipts or reputation do not replace enforceable rights.

### 6. Exit over lock-in

- People can pause, stop, delete, export or leave.
- Institutional access does not create behavioural coercion.
- No product or contract may punish someone for not disclosing an inner state.

## Stakeholder contracts

### People

STATE provides clear purpose, evidence status, limits, duration, cost and exit. It does not sell emotional profiles, create hidden vulnerability scores or pressure continued use.

### Employers

Employers may receive implementation support, privacy-safe aggregates and recommendations for healthier working conditions. They may not access names, individual states, notes, formula histories or inferred mental-health labels. Participation is separated from performance management.

### Schools

School programmes require age-appropriate design, safeguarding, student voice and safe refusal. Schools may not grade emotions, compel disclosure or use STATE as behavioural discipline.

### Health partners

Health programmes require clinical review, contraindications, escalation paths and accurate separation between prevention, support and treatment. STATE does not imply diagnosis, cure or treatment without the required evidence and legal basis.

### Creators and contributors

Creators know the scope, payment, acceptance test, attribution and rights before work begins. Incentives reward safety, usefulness, evidence quality and durable craft—not engagement or exaggerated claims.

### STATE

Revenue may come from consumer purchases, licences, implementation, training, evaluation and bounded partnerships. It may not depend on screen time, emotional surveillance, compulsory use, dark patterns or selling vulnerability.

## Metric constitution

### Suitable commercial metrics

- safe programme delivery;
- completed finite practices;
- real-world actions;
- equitable access;
- facilitator capability;
- validated outcome improvement;
- increasing self-sufficiency.

### Useful but dangerous proxies

- voluntary return;
- session completion;
- self-reported state shift;
- next-hour usefulness;
- team climate;
- absence and retention;
- wearable signals.

These require careful interpretation and must not be presented as causal proof without an appropriate study design.

### Metrics STATE refuses

- minutes watched;
- longest streak;
- mandatory participation;
- individual emotion dashboards for institutions;
- manager access to private notes;
- bonuses for stronger or more dramatic claims;
- revenue tied to dependency.

## Partnership red lines

STATE should not sign a deal when any of the following remains active:

- participation is coercive or linked to employment, grades, care access or reputation;
- the buyer demands identifiable emotional or practice data;
- payment depends on screen time, compulsory use or repeated engagement;
- the programme disguises preventable organisational harm as an individual resilience problem;
- evidence, safeguarding, complaints or exit responsibilities have no clear owner;
- commercial value is used to override a privacy, safety or human-rights concern.

## Governance and review

- Publish corrections and material challenges.
- Disclose major conflicts of interest.
- Include user and beneficiary representatives in programme design and review.
- Assign independent ethics, privacy and evidence review for higher-risk deployments.
- Revisit this charter whenever the business model, data model or institutional buyer changes.

## Economic constitution

STATE will not become rich by making people feel inadequate, exposing their vulnerability, or teaching them to endure conditions that should change.

STATE will build finite tools, fair collaborations and institutions capable of creating healthier bodies, calmer minds and more loving relationships.

When incentives drift, STATE discloses the conflict, changes the contract or walks away.
