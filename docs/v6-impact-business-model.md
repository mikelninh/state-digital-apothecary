# STATE V6 — Impact and Business Model

## Economic principle

STATE earns money by helping organisations create better conditions outside the product. It does not optimise for minutes watched, compulsive return or emotional dependency.

## Offer 1 — STATE for Teams

### Buyer
People, HR, occupational health, learning and development, or business leaders.

### Product
- personal finite formulas;
- team transition rituals;
- manager and facilitator guides;
- privacy-preserving aggregate reporting;
- optional research and implementation support.

### Illustrative pricing
- pilot: €15,000–€40,000 for 3–6 months;
- software licence: €4–€8 per eligible employee per month;
- enterprise implementation and evaluation: €20,000–€150,000;
- optional customised formulas: separately scoped.

At €5 per employee per month:

- 500 employees = €30,000 annual recurring revenue;
- 5,000 employees = €300,000 annual recurring revenue before volume discounts.

## Offer 2 — STATE for Schools

### Buyer
School authorities, foundations, municipalities, ministries, insurers or school networks.

### Product
- age-appropriate Body / Mind / Relationships curriculum;
- short classroom and transition practices;
- teacher and social-worker facilitation guides;
- safeguarding and accessibility;
- student voice and participatory evaluation;
- free family-access layer where funded.

### Illustrative pricing
- €12–€30 per student per year;
- single-school pilot: €8,000–€30,000;
- district or municipal programme: €40,000–€250,000;
- training and evaluation are separate line items.

## Offer 3 — STATE Health

### Buyer
Health insurers, hospitals, care providers, rehabilitation services, medical practices and public-health partners.

### Product
- staff recovery and transition formulas;
- prevention and health-literacy programmes;
- carefully bounded patient or waiting-period support;
- clinician-reviewed claims and contraindications;
- evaluation protocol and integration support.

### Illustrative pricing
- scoped pilot: €50,000–€250,000;
- regional insurer or provider programme: €250,000–€1,000,000+;
- per-active-participant or population contracts can be tested after outcomes and data governance are validated.

STATE is not positioned as treatment unless a later product completes the necessary clinical, regulatory and legal work.

## Offer 4 — STATE Commons / Communities

### Buyer
Municipalities, libraries, youth services, neighbourhood organisations, foundations and public programmes.

### Product
- free public formulas;
- facilitator training;
- local community pulse;
- family and relationship practices;
- open-source or publicly licensed components;
- transparent treasury and impact reporting.

### Illustrative pricing
- local annual programme: €25,000–€150,000;
- larger municipal or multi-site programme: €150,000–€750,000+;
- public-benefit access can be cross-subsidised by commercial customers.

## Consumer layer

- one formula: approximately €3;
- Human Starter Pack: €12;
- optional personal cabinet: €6–€12 per month;
- no required subscription for purchased formulas;
- gift and pay-it-forward access.

The consumer layer builds trust and product evidence. Institutional contracts can finance wider access and more rigorous evaluation.

## Illustrative growth scenarios — not forecasts

### Year 1: prove delivery
- three workplace pilots averaging €25,000 = €75,000;
- one school or community pilot = €50,000;
- one health or insurer pilot = €100,000;
- consumer and gifts = €25,000;
- illustrative revenue: €250,000.

### Year 2: repeatable programmes
- 15 employers averaging €60,000 = €900,000;
- five education/community programmes averaging €60,000 = €300,000;
- three health partners averaging €150,000 = €450,000;
- consumer and partnerships = €150,000;
- illustrative revenue: €1.8 million.

### Year 3: scaled institutional portfolio
- 70 employers averaging €75,000 = €5.25 million;
- 20 education/community programmes averaging €75,000 = €1.5 million;
- ten health/public partners averaging €250,000 = €2.5 million;
- consumer, gifts and creator formulas = €750,000;
- illustrative revenue: €10 million.

These figures are scenario arithmetic. They require strong product outcomes, procurement capability, privacy and security, source review, implementation teams and credible partners.

## Outcome framework

### Person
- state shift;
- observable action;
- next-hour usefulness;
- voluntary reuse;
- ability to create the condition without STATE.

### Family and relationships
- meaningful contact;
- appreciation or repair completed;
- perceived connection and safety;
- family routines created outside the app.

### Organisation
- participation without coercion;
- self-reported recovery, focus and connection;
- team-level behaviour or climate indicators;
- retention and absence data only with careful causal language and adequate study design.

### Community
- equitable reach;
- free access delivered and redeemed;
- facilitator capacity;
- local social connection and participation;
- transparent costs and outcomes.

## Anti-addiction commercial guardrail

No contract should reward STATE for increased screen time. Suitable commercial metrics include completed finite sessions, real-world actions, improvements in validated outcomes, access equity, successful programme delivery and increasing self-sufficiency.
