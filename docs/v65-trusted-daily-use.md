# STATE V6.5 — Trusted Daily Use

## Product hypothesis

STATE becomes trustworthy when the daily experience, the evidence loop and the commercial constitution reinforce one another.

> One finite condition. Honest delayed evidence. Transparent incentives. A real exit.

## Changes from V6.3 / V6.4

### Daily product + trust layer
- unobtrusive `Why trust STATE?` access from the daily product;
- six incentive-alignment commitments embedded in the product;
- explicit private-data contract and institutional red lines;
- the trust layer stays outside the practice room.

### Honest evidence loop
- immediate intensity and usefulness are recorded immediately;
- the next-hour usefulness question is no longer asked before the hour occurred;
- an unresolved session becomes eligible for delayed review after 60 minutes;
- delayed reviews remain local to the browser;
- migrated V6.3 records remain readable.

### Seven-day conditions lab
- days used, finite doses, average immediate shift and next-hour score;
- seven-day visual history without streak language;
- a genuine-use question distinguishes real need from prototype testing;
- missing a day is explicitly treated as information, not failure.

## Safety and privacy

- no account;
- no network request for personal records;
- no employer or manager access;
- no diagnosis or treatment claim;
- source links and limitations remain outside the finite practice;
- users can stop the practice at any time.

## Review questions

1. Is the state selection still immediate and clear?
2. Does the trust link reassure without interrupting the ritual?
3. Is the practice room still completely isolated?
4. Does delayed next-hour review produce more honest evidence?
5. Would the user open STATE for a real need rather than to test it?
6. Does seven-day learning feel informative rather than streak-driven?

## Next gate

Do not add another modality until Michael and at least five external testers have generated enough real-use sessions to identify whether CALM 10 and the state selector are genuinely useful.
