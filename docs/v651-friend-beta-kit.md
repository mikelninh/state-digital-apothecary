## What testers should evaluate

Do not ask whether the page is beautiful. Ask:

1. Did you think of STATE in a real moment without being reminded?
2. Could you select your state without explanation?
3. Did the recommendation feel appropriate?
4. Was the practice room calm and clear?
5. Could you pause or stop without guilt?
6. Did the immediate result differ from the next-hour result?
7. Would you voluntarily open it again?
8. What felt unsafe, manipulative, confusing or unnecessary?

## Device matrix

Aim to include:

- at least two iPhones;
- at least two Android phones;
- Safari and Chrome;
- one small-screen phone;
- one device with reduced-motion enabled;
- one private/incognito test to confirm the storage warning.

## Founder rules during the test

- Do not explain the interface unless someone is completely blocked.
- Do not defend design decisions.
- Record the tester’s exact words.
- Do not ask people to manufacture a mood for testing.
- Do not count a compliment as successful usage.
- Freeze feature development for the seven-day window.
- Fix only severe safety, data-loss or access blockers during the test.

## Decision after seven days

Choose only one:

- **Continue:** people used it voluntarily and core interaction held up.
- **Narrow:** one formula or moment worked; focus there.
- **Redesign:** people understood the idea but not the experience.
- **Stop:** the product did not create enough real-world value.
