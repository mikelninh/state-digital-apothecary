# FOCUS 25 — Initial Evidence Protocol

This is a lightweight product-learning protocol, not a clinical study.

## Goal

Learn whether FOCUS 25 helps a user begin and meaningfully advance one chosen task during the session, and whether the following hour feels more useful.

## First cohort

Five to ten consenting adult beta testers.

## Before the dose

Record:

- one concrete task outcome;
- current resistance or avoidance;
- current ability to focus;
- optional context note.

## During the dose

Observe without interrupting:

- whether preparation is understood;
- time required to begin;
- abandonment or pause points;
- unexpected friction.

## After the dose

Record:

- whether the task moved forward;
- resistance after the session;
- perceived focus;
- “Did this improve the next hour of your actual life?” score;
- one qualitative comment.

## Decision rules

Do not expand the product catalogue until the flagship experience is clear and useful.

Prioritise:

1. safety or distress reports;
2. users unable to understand the next action;
3. abandonment before work begins;
4. weak or inconsistent outcome improvement;
5. visual polish and delight.

## Reporting

Publish the sample size and limitations. Do not market small beta results as scientific proof.
