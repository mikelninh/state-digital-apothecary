# STATE Vision

STATE is a concept store for intentionally designed human states.

People do not only buy content. They seek focus, relief, courage, connection, play, awe, agency and meaning. Most digital products monetise those needs by keeping people inside a loop. STATE aims to deliver the desired state and return the person to life.

## Flagship formulas

- **FOCUS 25** — move one meaningful task into motion
- **CALM 10** — regulate before reacting
- **COURAGE 01** — complete one small brave action
- **TWO QUESTIONS** — replace small talk with a real conversation
- **ONE GOOD DEED** — turn goodwill into visible contribution
- **PLAY 08** — create without instrumentalising joy

## Business model

- individual finite doses;
- themed ritual packs;
- live facilitated experiences;
- ethical creator formulas;
- institutional access;
- “buy one, fund one” sponsored access.

## Long-term platform

A trusted marketplace and evidence layer where creators design finite experiences, users understand effects and limitations, and public-interest formulas can be openly shared.

The north-star question remains:

> Did this experience improve the next hour of your actual life?
