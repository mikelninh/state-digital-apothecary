# STATE V6.3 — Daily Dose

## Purpose

V6.3 is the first prototype designed for ordinary repeat use rather than brand exploration.

> One state selector. One finite condition. One beautiful exit.

## Core journey

1. **Arrive** — choose one clear state card.
2. **Recognise** — ritual mode acknowledges the state before advice.
3. **Recommend** — STATE offers one condition, not a feed.
4. **Choose dose** — quick 60 seconds or the full formula duration.
5. **Practise** — all page and science content disappears into a dedicated practice room.
6. **Reflect** — record an honest before/after state and next-hour usefulness.
7. **Leave** — no next recommendation, upsell, streak or social prompt.

## Interaction fixes from V6.2

- state options are large, explicit buttons rather than floating decorative labels;
- selected state receives a strong visual state and confirmation;
- Continue remains disabled until a choice is made;
- `Something else` supports a private free-text description;
- practice runs in a fully isolated fullscreen layer;
- marketing copy, science panels and packaging never overlap the dose;
- pause, early completion and gentle exit remain available;
- mobile and reduced-motion layouts are included.

## Two modes

### Ritual

Recognition and a warm spatial transition appear before the formula. Intended for first use or moments where the person wants more care and atmosphere.

### Quick

The user moves from state selection directly to the dose recommendation. Intended for familiar daily use.

## Formula mapping

- Overwhelmed → CALM 10
- Scattered → FOCUS 25
- Tense → RELEASE 05
- Flat → WALK 20
- Avoiding → COURAGE 01
- Lonely → CONNECT 02
- Something else → PAUSE 02

CALM 10 is the flagship guided-exhale experience. Other formulas use a bounded one-minute threshold in the prototype and retain their intended full duration.

## Evidence boundaries

- mechanisms are described cautiously;
- science sources remain optional and outside the practice room;
- STATE does not claim to measure hormones, neurotransmitters or brain activation;
- personal ratings are private observations, not medical outcomes;
- no formula is presented as diagnosis, treatment or crisis support.

## Local records

Completed reflections are stored only in the current browser under `state-v63-sessions`. No account, upload or community sharing occurs.

## Test criteria

The version is successful when:

- state choice is understandable without explanation;
- a quick dose can begin within a few seconds;
- the practice screen has no visual competition;
- users understand how to pause and stop;
- people can report no improvement without friction;
- the ending makes leaving the screen feel like completion;
- voluntary repeat use occurs in real daily situations.

## Next validation

- founder dogfooding for seven days;
- five external testers, at least three sessions each;
- qualitative observation before adding accounts, wearables, community or further formulas.
