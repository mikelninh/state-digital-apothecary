# STATE number system

STATE numbers are semantic dose codes rather than one universal unit.

- **CALM 10** — ten-minute full dose.
- **FOCUS 25** — twenty-five-minute full dose.
- **COURAGE 01** — one brave, observable act. Full ritual: fifteen minutes.
- **CONNECTION 02 / TWO QUESTIONS** — two mutual questions. Full ritual: twenty minutes.
- **PURPOSE 01 / ONE GOOD DEED** — one bounded useful contribution. Full ritual: thirty minutes.
- **HUMAN 05** — five formulas in the Human Starter Pack.

## Packaging rule

The large number communicates the memorable product idea. A smaller label must always explain its unit:

- `10 MINUTES`
- `ONE BRAVE ACT · 15 MIN`
- `TWO QUESTIONS · 20 MIN`
- `ONE GOOD DEED · 30 MIN`
- `FIVE FORMULAS`

This keeps the naming expressive without forcing users to decode it.
