# STATE Ethical Constitution

STATE borrows the potency and ritual of a “digital drug store” while rejecting the extractive mechanics of addictive platforms.

## Non-negotiable principles

### 1. The product succeeds when it ends
Every formula must have a beginning, a finite dose and an invitation to leave.

### 2. No manufactured dependency
No endless feeds, punishment-based streaks, guilt notifications, fake emergencies or emotional manipulation.

### 3. Honest relationships
Clearly disclose what is automated, human-created, personalised, sponsored or experimental.

### 4. Consent and control
Users choose the state, intensity and duration. They can stop at any time and retain control of their data.

### 5. Outcome over engagement
Primary measurement: whether the next hour of real life improved. Screen time is not a success metric.

### 6. Evidence before claims
Start at zero. Publish sample sizes, limitations, negative results and changes made because of evidence.

### 7. Abundance without coercion
Paid products may fund free access, but generosity must never become guilt-based conversion pressure.

### 8. Public-interest commons
Selected low-risk formulas should be available for schools, carers, NGOs and public institutions to adapt responsibly.
