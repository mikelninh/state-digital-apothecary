# STATE Roadmap — V4 to V7

> Do not seek to be impressive. Seek to be genuinely useful — and make usefulness beautiful.

This roadmap deliberately progresses from **one useful experience** to **shared public infrastructure**. Each version must earn the right to add complexity.

## V4 — The First Real Dose

**Question:** Can one finite digital experience reliably help a person move one meaningful task forward?

### Build
1. Refine FOCUS 25 into a complete before → prepare → dose → after → return ritual.
2. Make the core experience work beautifully on mobile and desktop.
3. Keep data local by default; make export voluntary.
4. Produce a local proof receipt for each completed or honestly abandoned session.
5. Add accessible keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support.
6. Conduct five internal sessions, then thirty external beta sessions.

### Evidence gates
- At least 70% session completion.
- Average next-hour score of at least 4/5 across a meaningful beta sample.
- Users can identify what specifically helped.
- At least three observed friction points are fixed before V5.

### Do not add yet
Accounts, subscriptions, social feeds, tokens, open creator publishing or clinical claims.

## V5 — The Human Starter Pack

**Question:** Can STATE become a useful, purchasable and giftable product without becoming manipulative?

### Build
1. Add CALM 10, COURAGE 01, CONNECTION 02 and PURPOSE 01.
2. Give every formula its own interaction model rather than recolouring FOCUS.
3. Launch the Human Starter Pack for €12.
4. Add gifting and redemption codes.
5. Implement a real “buy one, fund one” ledger.
6. Launch STATE Commons with free foundation formulas and facilitator guides.
7. Introduce privacy-preserving accounts only when cross-device access becomes valuable.

### Evidence gates
- Each formula meets its own success metric.
- The donation ledger reconciles money received, access funded and access redeemed.
- At least one school, NGO, carer group or community partner tests a Commons formula.
- Repeat use is voluntary and useful, not driven by streak pressure.

---

## V6 — The Formula Studio & Contribution Network

**Question:** Can trusted specialists and communities create useful formulas while quality and ethics remain intact?

### Build
1. Create an invite-only Formula Studio.
2. Provide a formula schema: intended state, mechanism, duration, contraindications, evidence, completion condition and aftercare.
3. Add structured peer review and ethics review.
4. Add transparent creator revenue sharing.
5. Create contribution roles: researcher, designer, facilitator, translator, tester, reviewer and sponsor.
6. Issue non-transferable contribution receipts for verified work.
7. Pilot project-specific shared ownership through legal agreements, not speculative tokens.

### Evidence gates
- Three external formulas pass review and produce useful beta outcomes.
- Contributors understand exactly what they own and what they do not.
- No contributor income depends on artificial engagement or emotional dependency.
- Governance has a tested conflict and removal process.

---

## V7 — STATE Commons Protocol & Civic Infrastructure

**Question:** Can STATE operate as trusted infrastructure for human flourishing and coordinated public action?

### Build
1. Publish an open formula protocol and interoperability standard.
2. Establish a mission-based cooperative or steward-owned entity.
3. Introduce transparent participatory budgeting for Commons funds.
4. Launch STATE CIVIC formulas: understand, deliberate, contribute and verify.
5. Add public-good project boards with scoped missions, budgets, deliverables and proof.
6. Allow contributors to earn project-specific economic participation where legally valid.
7. Create independent ethics, evidence and treasury councils.
8. Make governance progressively decentralised only after the underlying institutions work.

### Evidence gates
- Multiple independent organisations run compatible formulas.
- Treasury and impact reporting are publicly auditable.
- Governance participation is broad enough that no founder or wealthy holder can dominate.
- The system has survived at least one real dispute, failed proposal and governance correction.

# Release discipline

Every version follows the same loop:

1. **Hypothesis** — name the human outcome.
2. **Smallest useful build** — remove everything not needed to test it.
3. **Private pilot** — observe real behaviour.
4. **Evidence review** — include failures and abandonment.
5. **Ethics review** — identify pressure, dependency and power asymmetry.
6. **Public beta** — publish limitations with results.
7. **Release gate** — proceed only when usefulness is earned.

Complexity is not progress. More ownership mechanics are not decentralisation. A DAO is useful only when it gives real people clearer rights, trustworthy coordination and accountable power.
