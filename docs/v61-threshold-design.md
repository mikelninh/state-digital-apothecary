# STATE V6.1 — The Threshold

## Product thesis

A place people love returning to, but never get trapped inside.

V6.1 concentrates STATE into one signature journey:

1. arrive honestly;
2. feel recognised before receiving advice;
3. enter one relevant room;
4. receive one finite formula;
5. understand its experience, evidence and limits;
6. practise;
7. leave STATE and use the next hour.

## Art direction — Living Sanctuary

The visual world combines:

- the warmth and materiality of a home;
- the ritual clarity of an apothecary;
- the stillness of a contemplative space;
- the precision of a scientific instrument.

It avoids generic corporate wellness, clinical sterility, spiritual ornament without context, and reward-driven app aesthetics.

## Signature visual language

### Body
Sunlight, clay, warmth, ground, pulse and physical rhythm.

### Mind
Water, glass, breath, spacious light, narrowing attention and quiet movement.

### Relationships
Two seats, shared warmth, threads, thresholds and reciprocal space.

### Evidence
Science, wisdom traditions and personal evidence remain visually and conceptually distinct.

## Interaction principles

- recognition comes before prescription;
- one room and one formula are prioritised;
- information is layered rather than displayed all at once;
- motion breathes, settles, opens and recedes;
- every practice is finite;
- the ending becomes visually quieter;
- no next-product recommendation appears after a completed dose;
- absence and self-sufficiency count as success.

## First prototype paths

- overwhelmed → CALM 10;
- physically tense → RELEASE 05;
- scattered → FOCUS 25;
- flat → WALK 20;
- avoiding something → COURAGE 01;
- lonely → CONNECT 02.

CALM 10 is the flagship fully art-directed practice. The other one-minute thresholds test the broader recommendation and room system.

## Source boundary

The interface may explain plausible systems, but it must not imply that STATE measured hormones, neurotransmitters, brainwaves or regional brain activation. A source card distinguishes:

- bounded scientific claims;
- traditional or contemplative context;
- what STATE measured personally;
- what remains unmeasured.

## Release gate

Test the first 90 seconds and one completed practice with at least five people.

The experience advances only when most testers can say:

- I knew what to do without instruction from another person;
- the experience felt distinct rather than like a wellness landing page;
- I felt understood rather than categorised;
- I trusted the separation of evidence, tradition and personal data;
- the ending genuinely made me want to leave the screen;
- I would choose to return on another day.

## Success question

Did the person close STATE with a clearer ability to create the condition in real life?
