# STATE Brand World 01

> Do not seek to be impressive. Seek to be genuinely useful — and make usefulness beautiful.

## Brand idea

STATE is a **digital apothecary for human flourishing**. Its visual world combines premium editorial design, clinical packaging clarity, concept-store tactility, human ritual and playful scepticism toward addictive technology.

STATE should never look like a gambling product, productivity cult, supplement scam or generic wellness dashboard.

## Brand architecture

- **STATE** — master brand and digital apothecary.
- **STATE Formulas** — finite experiences such as FOCUS 25 and CALM 10.
- **STATE Studio** — invite-only contribution and creation system.
- **STATE Commons** — open public-good formulas, funding and civic missions.
- **STATE Civic** — a later application layer for understanding, dialogue and coordinated action.

## Visual system

### Neutrals

- Ink `#10120E`
- Bone `#F2EEE3`
- Paper `#FBF8F0`
- Acid signal `#D8FF49`

### Formula spectrum

- FOCUS `#FF6545`
- CALM `#91C9FF`
- COURAGE `#EEA7EF`
- CONNECTION `#F1D96D`
- PURPOSE `#94D5A8`

Colour is semantic. Formula colour identifies the emotional job and guides interaction rather than merely decorating a section.

## Typography

- System sans: instructions, navigation and product clarity.
- Editorial serif: humanity, reflection, quotations and the emotional promise.
- Monospace: formula IDs, timing, evidence, receipts and operational truth.

## Packaging rules

Every formula package should expose:

1. master brand;
2. formula name;
3. memorable numeric dose;
4. duration;
5. intended outcome;
6. boundaries and ethical label;
7. evidence status.

The pack is a metaphorical object that makes a digital experience feel deliberate, finite and giftable.

## Motion

- breathe, orbit, reveal and settle;
- no casino bounce or reward explosions;
- motion should slow attention rather than capture it;
- every experience respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Voice

STATE is direct, warm, playful, ambitious and evidence-aware. It is never cold, sentimental, flippant, grandiose or falsely clinical.

Useful lines:

- Human states, consciously designed.
- Digital drugs. No hangover.
- The product succeeds when you close it.
- Evidence starts at zero.
- Paused without punishment.
- Useful first. Beautiful always.
- Ownership only when it is real.

## Anti-patterns

Do not use false medical claims, neurochemical certainty as decoration, countdown pressure, streak guilt, follower counts as legitimacy, token language before rights exist, generic gradients without purpose or sterile hospital aesthetics without humanity.
